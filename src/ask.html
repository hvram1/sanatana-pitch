How we thank you

Your name stays off the verse, and one click away from it

The texts belong to the tradition, so no sponsor's name is printed on a verse, a chapter or a recording. Each result you fund instead carries one small link to the sponsors' page, where you are thanked by name.

On every page you funded

A single line at the foot of the chapter or text:

This edition was sponsored · see sponsors
On the sponsors' page

Your name, what you funded, when, and links to each result. Anonymous if you prefer.

In the meter

The figure on this page moves as the work is done, and it is printed by the sites themselves. You can check progress without asking us.

Why you can trust the prices

One figure is measured; the rest use these rates

Turning a recording into an edition you can play verse by verse costs ₹49 an hour of audio. That is measured on 93 hours of real recordings, not estimated. Almost all of the cost of this work is people, and the trust began on the premise that the vidvāns and scholars are honoured for their work. Vidvāns and scholars who carry a project are retained by the month; senior vidvāns consulted on a project are paid by the hour; students do much of the reading. We priced every line with these rates, at the top of each range.

Who we are

Sanatana Sampatti Foundation

A registered trust in Srirangapatna, founded in June 2022. Trustees: Dr. C. S. Yogananda, Veda Brahmashree Bhanuprakasha Sharma and Sri Harihara Vinayakram. Sanatana.in is developed under the guidance of the Jagadguru Śaṅkarācārya of Sringeri. Donations qualify for deduction under section 80G of the Income Tax Act.

To fund a line, or to talk it through, write to secretary@sanatanasampatti.in.

Funding from a company's CSR budget? The trust is registered for CSR (CSR00111219). See eligibility and the Schedule VII item for each result.
