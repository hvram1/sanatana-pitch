**Subject:** A handful of scholars, India's oldest texts, and Claude Code

Sanatana Sampatti Foundation is a small trust in Srirangapatna, Karnataka.
We put the foundational texts of Sanātana Dharma, their commentaries and
recordings of their recitation online, in a form anyone can read, search and
listen to.

Like most such efforts in India, we are a handful of people. In the last year,
Claude Code has worked in our team as engineer, editor, designer, analyst and
the one who writes our funding case. We now have nine sites open to anyone:
the Veda with the text following the chant word by word, 8,391 Purāṇa verses
heard at the second a teacher chants them in a Tamil discourse, Śaṅkara's
commentaries with Tamil and English side by side, and a verse index of
513,887 verses that every site checks against. The scholars still decide what
is correct. Claude Code makes their judgement go further.

The story is here, in a few minutes of scrolling: **[link to the showcase page]**

We think it is a good example of what Claude can do for the many small groups
that keep India's traditions alive, in every language. We would like to ask
three things, in this order:

1. **Tell the story.** As a customer story, a post, or part of your India
   launch. We will help with anything you need: recordings, screenshots,
   interviews.
2. **Claude credits** for the team, and for the pāṭhaśālā students and
   vidvāns who verify the work as it grows.
3. **Fund one named result**, such as the first index of names for any Purāṇa
   (₹37.5 lakh) or a chanting tutor for pāṭhaśālās (₹14 lakh). Every result we
   can deliver, with its price and timeline, is here: **[link to the funding
